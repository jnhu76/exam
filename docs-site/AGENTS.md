# docs-site local rules

Local constraints for this directory; the root `AGENTS.md` remains the semantic
authority for the repository.

- `content/` is generated by `scripts/project-content.mjs` and must never be
  edited or committed. It is gitignored.
- Product and guide text is owned by `docs/product/` and `docs/guides/`. Edit the
  canonical file, never a projected copy.
- Publishing a new canonical document requires an explicit entry in
  `scripts/site-model.mjs` (allowlist) and the source-to-route map in `README.md`.
- Site-authored pages under `pages/` may add navigation and context, not new
  product capabilities, permissions, guarantees, or save/recovery claims.
- Keep the site repo-only. Do not add Pages/Wiki workflows, hosting, analytics,
  third-party search, accounts, or feedback services without a new owner decision.
