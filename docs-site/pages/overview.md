# Overview

Exam is a general-purpose exam and assessment platform. Its supported deployment
shape, applicability, single-organization boundary, and language boundary are
stated in [Standard capabilities §1–§2](/standard/capabilities#_1-applicability).
This page is a router; it does not restate product guarantees.

## What does Standard support today?

The authoritative answer is the version-bound
[support & evidence matrix](/standard/capabilities#_8-support-evidence-matrix).
This page does not summarize it: the matrix carries the product status,
preconditions, applicable version, evidence type, normative source, and
limitations for each row.

## What are the known limits?

Read the [explicit non-guarantees](/standard/capabilities#_7-explicit-non-guarantees)
and [applicability](/standard/capabilities#_1-applicability). They are the only
places where limits are stated.
